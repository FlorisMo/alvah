### Humane Fact Dojo — spaced fluency, race your past self
`engine 01` · `Model A` · bands 8–18 · seed: 01 §5
**Loop.** Short daily bursts of spaced retrieval; missed items resurface sooner; the display is a
fluency map filling in, never a clock or a leaderboard.
**Grows (L7, L10).** 8–10 number bonds to 20, subitising · 10–12 tables via arrays (groups-of before
grind) · 12–14 fraction↔percent↔decimal equivalents — **this deck feeds the Percent Forge gate**
(doc 02 §4) · 14–16 estimation anchors (√, powers, unit conversions) + algebra identities ·
16–18 **self-chosen decks** — he decides what is worth automating (bird Latin names, chords, code
syntax); the dojo becomes his own tool (authorship flip).
**Varies.** Deck themes as data: antler pairs, seed counts, kiosk receipts (finance seam), species
names (perception seam). Ghost mode: race the answer-speed of your own last month, gently.
**Flags.** Spaced + low-stakes is the *zeker* part; timed shaming is vetoed (doc 01 §7 Boaler line —
humane fluency, neither high-stakes drills nor no-practice).

### Number-Bond Builder → Bar-Model Story Solver — the CPA ladder
`engine 01 → 03` · `A then B` · bands 8–14 · seeds: 01 §5
**Loop.** Bonds: see 7 as 5+2 with manipulables. Bar models: draw the bar that *is* the word problem,
then read the answer off the drawing.
**Grows (L7, L4).** 8–9 part-whole with objects · 9–11 bar models for +/− stories · 11–13 fraction
and ratio bars · 12–14 bars that quietly *are* algebra ("the bar you don't know yet is x") — the
symbol arrives after the picture already made it obvious.
**Varies.** Story skins from the reserve (feed rations, plank lengths, visitor counts); child-authored
problems for the mentor to solve (early authorship flip).
**Flags.** Never symbol-first before the pictorial stage lands (doc 01 §3, *zeker*).

### Sort-and-Rule — deduce the hidden rule
`engine 01 (recognition) + 03 (production)` · `A+B` · bands 8–18 · seed: 01 §5
**Loop.** Items sort themselves by a hidden rule; the child tests guesses by placing new items, then
names the rule.
**Grows (L2, L4, L10).** 8–10 one attribute (colour, size) · 10–12 two attributes, nested rules ·
12–14 **minimal-queries mode** — deduce the rule in the fewest placements (information-value
intuition) · 14–16 adversarial: *he designs* rules that resist the mentor's guessing · 16–18
quantified claims ("all", "some", "none") about the sets — proof-adjacent language.
**Varies.** Sorting tracks, leaves, calls, weather records; a "mystery warden" NPC whose sorting rule
changed overnight (what changed?).
**Flags.** Conditional/classification reasoning is realistic young *with concrete stimuli* (doc 01 §3).

### If-Then Machines — build rule chains; meet validity vs truth
`engine 03` · `Model B (rung-gated)` · bands 8–18 · seed: 01 §5
**Loop.** Snap together if-then gates so inputs (water, seeds, animals) route correctly through a
contraption; run it and watch.
**Grows (L7).** 8–10 single rule · 10–12 chains of 2–3 rules · 12–15 the flagship beat: a machine
that is **perfectly valid but produces a false output** because a premise was wrong — validity ≠
truth, felt (doc 01 §3, the 12–15 rung) · 14–16 and/or/not composition, early quantifiers · 16–18
mini-proofs: "show this machine *must* do X for every input."
**Varies.** Water-routing, seed-sorting, gate-and-fence logic; "machine poetry" (silly rule chains) as
a creativity crossover.
**Flags.** Abstract necessity before ~11 is pushed-too-early (doc 01 §3); keep it enactive first.

### Fix the Machine — debugging as a life skill
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 10–18 · new — design hypothesis (grows out of If-Then Machines)
**Loop.** A machine (or plan, or argument) misbehaves; find the *one* broken rule from its observed
behaviour, fix it, and prove the fix with a test input.
**Grows (L4, L10).** 10–12 which of two rules broke? · 12–14 **choose the fewest test inputs that
localise the fault** (binary-search intuition, transferable to real debugging) · 14–16 debug a
*plan* or an *argument* — the same move applied to a milestone list or a claim chain (Argument Map
seam) · 16–18 debug the in-fiction assistant's multi-step work: two planted flaws, find both before
approving (the verify half of [Delegate or Do?](area-04-work-and-agency.md)).
**Varies.** Broken irrigation, mis-sorted seed shed, a faulty route plan, a flawed newsletter argument.
**Flags.** Design hypothesis — check against doc 01 §7 (no general-transfer claims; each domain is
its own skill).

### One-Thing-At-A-Time (CVS) — fair tests only
`engine 01` · `Model A` · bands 10–18 · seed: 01 §5
**Loop.** Design a test where only one thing varies; unfair tests visibly prove nothing.
**Grows (L4, L9).** 10–12 spot the fair test among options · 12–14 design it, name the confound ·
14–16 read a claimed "study" from the reserve newsletter and find what wasn't controlled · 16–18
design a real n=1 experiment on his own practice or fuel (seams: [The Training Plan](area-04-work-and-agency.md),
[The Fuel Test](area-09-health-and-attention.md)).
**Varies.** Which bait, which soil, which wax on the skis, which practice schedule.
**Flags.** CVS is the *zeker* anchor of this area (doc 01 §4); keep it the workhorse.

### Cause Detective — cause vs coincidence
`engine 01 (choice) + 03 (design)` · `A+B` · bands 8–18 · seed: 01 §5
**Loop.** Two things co-occur (boars rooting + fence damage); design the tiny test that isolates
whether one causes the other.
**Grows (L7).** 8–10 pick which of two explanations a clue supports · 10–12 first isolation tests ·
12–14 confounds, "what else changed?" · 14–16 correlation traps in charts and headlines · 16–18
simple causal diagrams (arrows), and reading a real study's design critically.
**Varies.** Field mysteries per season; a "coincidence of the week" feed.
**Flags.** Intuitive early, formal CVS later (doc 01 §3); pairs with the Sure-O-Meter (how sure is
the verdict?).

### Counterexample Hunt — why must it be true?
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 9–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** The game asserts a rule ("every red-chested bird here is a robin"); the child hunts the
scene for the counterexample — or, if none exists, argues why none *can*.
**Grows (L7, L10).** 9–11 spot the exception visually · 11–13 construct a counterexample to a stated
rule · 13–15 the pivot: some rules have no counterexample — feel the difference between "I didn't
find one" and "there can't be one" (necessity vs observation, doc 01 §3's borderline rung, landed
gently) · 16–18 produce the mini-proof or the counterexample, his choice of attack.
**Varies.** Nature rules, number rules ("every even number > 2 is a sum of two odd ones?"), rule
claims from NPCs who are confidently wrong (epistemics seam).
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; the necessity rung is exactly where doc 01 warns against pushing too
early — gate on readiness, not age.

### Guess the Forest — Fermi estimation
`engine 02 (quantity-predict)` · `Model A` · bands 8–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** Estimate a countable thing (acorns under the oak, visitors last summer, litres in the
pond), commit, then the reveal — with the *method* shown, not just the number.
**Grows (L1, L7, L10).** 8–10 closer/farther guessing with a visual anchor ("more than this bucket?")
· 10–12 order-of-magnitude bins (tens? hundreds? thousands?) · 12–14 decompose into two factors
(trees × acorns-per-tree) · 14–16 full Fermi chains plus a stated confidence interval (Sure-O-Meter
seam — interval elicitation) · 16–18 the applied payoff: **sanity-check an AI's or a headline's
number by Fermi** before trusting it.
**Varies.** Season counts, distances, weights, durations; "estimate then measure" field trips
(hybrid-real, L9).
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; estimation folklore is not the evidence base — anchor the mechanic in
number-sense pedagogy (doc 01 §4 CPA/number-talks) and the calibration machinery (engine 02).

### Evidence Tipper — belief that moves by degrees
`engine 03 + 02 layer` · `B (+A)` · bands 9–18 · seed: 01 §5
**Loop.** A counts-jar tips toward hypotheses as evidence arrives; predict where it lands, watch the
counts shift, update.
**Grows (L1, L7).** 9–11 two jars, visible counts (natural frequencies only — doc 01 §3) · 11–13
**Base-Rate Meadow** variant: the background rate sits on the table before any clue arrives ("how
common, normally?") · 13–15 evidence *strength* — one track vs a den with cubs · 16–18 verbal
likelihood-ratio intuition ("how much more likely under A than under B?"), still no formulas.
**Varies.** Whodunit skins (what raided the nest), weather jars, medical-style screening stories
(late band, where base-rate neglect classically bites).
**Flags.** Natural frequencies are the *zeker* framing; percentage framing too early is the known
trap (doc 01 §3).

### Argument Map Studio — see the skeleton of a claim
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 10–18 · seed: 01 §5
**Loop.** Lay out claim, evidence, and the connecting "because" as a visual map; wobbly links wobble.
**Grows (L4, L10).** 10–12 match given evidence to given claims (arrows only) · 12–14 build a map
from a short spoken argument · 14–16 find the **unstated assumption** — the invisible plank the map
needs · 16–18 map a live disagreement fairly (feeds [When Experts Disagree](area-02-epistemics.md)),
then *his own* position before a hard choice.
**Varies.** Reserve debates, newsletter letters, historical wolf-return arguments.
**Flags.** Argument mapping is *waarschijnlijk*, mostly adult-evidenced (doc 01 §4) — it stays a bet,
flagged in the open decisions there.
**A11y.** Fully drag-and-audio; no writing required — spoken claims land as tiles (doc 01 §8).

### Fallacy Zoo — meet the flawed moves as creatures
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 11–18 · seed: 01 §5
**Loop.** Each creature *is* a reasoning move gone wrong (the Strawman scarecrow, the Slippery Slope
otter); meet them through examples first, names later.
**Grows (L2).** 11–13 meet 4–5 creatures in stories · 13–15 spot them in the wild (newsletter,
overheard NPC rows) · 15–17 catch one mid-conversation (Engine 04 seam, if unblocked) · 16–18 the
honest rung: catch the creature **in your own argument** during a Studio session (self-critique seam).
**Varies.** New creatures unlock by season; a field guide page per creature (almanac artefact).
**Flags.** Fallacy-*naming* ≠ thinking well; transfer weak — teach via examples, keep expectations
modest (doc 01 §7).

## Cross-area seams (build once)

- **Calibration everywhere** — every verdict-shaped answer here can carry a Sure-O-Meter elicitation
  (area 02 owns the machinery; engine 02 composes with 01/03).
- **Percent Forge dependency** — the Fact Dojo's 12–14 deck is the on-ramp to finance's gate (doc 02 §4).
- **Fair tests → self-experiments** — CVS grows into the Training Plan / Fuel Test n=1 experiments
  (areas 04/09), which is where "logic" quietly becomes "learning to learn."
- **Debugging → verification** — Fix the Machine's late band is the same muscle as Delegate-or-Do's
  "check the assistant's work" (area 04) and Spot the Confident Robot (area 02).
