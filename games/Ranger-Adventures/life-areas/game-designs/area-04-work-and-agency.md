### The capture-to-finish spine (the tension→action loop, made playable)
Five seeds form one pipeline; their growth is one story, so they are laddered together.

**Notice-and-Name / Itch Log** `03·B` — wander, tap what bugs you, it lands in the inbox; later
clarify: project / reference / discard. **Scope-the-Restoration** — cut the itch to a one-sentence
story + smallest version. **Definition-of-Done Gate** — set the binary checkable done *before*
starting; the gate literally won't open until it's true. **Milestone-Marker** — 2–5 stakes along the
trail, milestone-1 reachable *now*. **Reserve Almanac** — the before/after photo pair closes the
loop; the almanac page **is** the reward.

**Grows (L4, L5, L6).**
- **8–10:** one itch at a time; scope = pick from two pre-scoped options; done-states pre-written,
  he *checks* them; milestone pre-placed; the system holds every step (doc 03 §3 — the game is his
  prospective memory).
- **10–12:** clarify his own inbox weekly; scope from a template ("As a ranger I want ___ so that
  ___"); write the done-state himself, mentor verifies it's checkable; place milestone-1 so it's
  reachable today.
- **12–14:** 2–4 week projects, 2–5 milestones; the **hybrid-real lane opens** — one real-life itch
  (his room, a school thing) runs on the same board, parent as reviewer (doc 03 §8).
- **14–16:** parallel projects under the Toolshed's WIP cap; learning-goals named before outcome
  goals for anything new (doc 03 §4); scope *defended* ("why this small?").
- **16–18:** the board quietly becomes a real personal system (generic capture/next-action/done —
  the de-trademarked workflow, doc 03 §9); the game is the review-and-almanac layer only.

**Varies.** Itch sources rotate (storm damage season, visitor complaints, his own ideas); scope
duels (mentor and ranger scope the same itch, compare cuts).
**Flags.** Conversion-rate of itches→finished is tracked as *presence*, never a point total; stale
itches retire without shame (the Weekly Walk's re-scope/retire beat).

### Weekly Reserve Walk — the review ritual
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 8–18 · seed: 03 §5
**Loop.** A guided walk past every live project: mark progress, re-scope, retire, pick the week's
one thing. The calm heartbeat of the whole area.
**Grows (L4, L8).** 8–10 three questions, parent co-walks · 10–12 solo walk, mentor asks the
questions · 12–14 he asks the questions; the walk gains a look-back beat ("what did last-week-me
not see?") · 14–16 full parallel-project review + Predict-Then-Check resolutions · 16–18 the walk
*is* his weekly review of real life; the reserve is the metaphor that stays.
**Varies.** Season walks (year-scale look-backs at chapter close); a two-minute storm-check variant
after surprises.

### If-Then Ranger Cards + Two-Minute Path — the executive glue
`03·B` · bands 8–18 · seeds: 03 §5
**If-Then cards** pre-decide the plan's weak moments ("if it rains, then I sort seeds indoors");
when the trigger fires in-world, the card surfaces itself. *Grows:* pick from a deck → author his
own → 14+ author *anti-derail* cards for real work ("if I open the tablet for maths and see a game,
then…" — attention seam, area 09). Implementation intentions are the area's best-evidenced tool for
under-12s (d≈0.43, doc 03 §4) — this card set is deliberately everywhere.
**Two-Minute Path** teaches the judgement call "is this a two-minute job?" — do it now, watch the
trail visibly clear. *Grows:* the game flags candidates → he flags them → he notices the *mis*-flags
(two-minute jobs that were secretly projects — the honest lesson).

### WIP-Limited Toolshed — finishing beats starting
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 13–18 · seed: 03 §5
**Loop.** N tool-pegs; no new project until one finishes and frees a peg. Throughput (finished ÷
started) and the Done Meadow make finishing visible.
**Grows (L6).** 13–14 two pegs · 14–16 three pegs + one reserved "slow peg" for a long project ·
16–18 he sets his own cap and defends it (the cap as self-knowledge, not rule).
**Flags.** The WIP cap doubles as the cognitive-load cap (doc 03 §9); unlimited parallelism is the
known failure mode (doc 03 §3).

### Help-Fade Mentor + Re-scope Rescue — scaffolds that retire themselves
`03·B` · seeds: 03 §5
The mentor gives less help each time a task-type succeeds; "you don't need me here" is a milestone
that *removes* a scaffold (informational, doc 03 §7). Re-scope Rescue catches stalls: "shrink the
done-state to something finishable this week?" *Grows:* suggested smaller scope → he proposes it →
he catches the stall himself before the game does (the real graduation signal, doc 03 §8).
**Flags.** Fade on demonstrated independence, never on a clock; recoveries are logged as wins
(re-scoping is skill, not failure).

### Season-Long Stewardship — the long horizon, earned
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 15–18 · seed: 03 §5 (hard-gated to the ~15–16 readiness band)
**Loop.** Adopt a corner of the reserve for a whole season: self-set milestones, monthly self-review,
a season almanac chapter.
**Grows (L5, L9).** 15–16 one corner, mentor reviews monthly · 16–18 a real long project (exam prep,
a build, a trip he plans) runs beside it on the same structure; the sim corner becomes optional.
**Flags.** The gate is the point: multi-month ownership before future-orientation matures produces
abandoned goals, not agency (doc 03 §1–3).

### Edge-of-Ability Trail — deliberate practice that feels like play
`engine 01` · `Model A` · bands 8–18 · seed: 03 §5–§6 (the area flagship)
**Loop.** A buildable skill (dry-stone walling, knots, whittling patterns) always offers the rung one
step past the last success; instant task-level feedback per placement; auto-tunes to ~70–85% success.
**Grows (L3, L10).** 8–10 the staircase does everything · 10–12 he picks *which* sub-skill to push
(SDT autonomy, doc 03 §6) · 12–14 interleaved variation (mixed wall sections — desirable
difficulty) · 14–16 delayed re-tests decide "mastered", not in-session smoothness (the fluency-
illusion guard, doc 03 §6) · 16–18 he designs the drill (see The Training Plan).
**Varies.** Any craft skin: walls, knots, fire-lays, birdhouse joints; personal-best portfolio per
craft.

### Predict-Then-Check — effort calibration
`engine 02` · `Model A` · bands 10–18 · seed: 03 §5 (reuses the Sure-O-Meter, reskinned)
**Loop.** Before a task: "done by Friday?" with confidence. Afterwards: the gentle reveal —
prediction vs reality, curve over time.
**Grows (L1, L5).** 10–12 will-I-finish-today items · 12–14 duration estimates ("how many walks?") ·
14–16 the planning-fallacy lesson made visible (his own optimism curve, named kindly) · 16–18 he
pads real plans using his own measured bias — self-knowledge as a tool.
**Flags.** Never a punishment for optimism; the curve is a map (doc 01 §6 presentation rules apply).

### The Ranger's One Thing — prioritisation
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 8–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** From several worthy itches, pick the one that matters most *now*, and say why in one
sentence. The reserve visibly responds to good sequencing (the leak fixed before the storm).
**Grows (L4, L7).** 8–10 pick between two with an obvious difference · 10–12 three candidates;
"what breaks if it waits?" as the question · 12–14 impact × effort as a felt 2×2 (big-help/easy
first) · 14–16 sequencing under dependency ("the path needs the bridge first") · 16–18 the weekly
real-life One Thing, chosen and defended at the Walk.
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; grounded in doc 03's proximal-goals evidence and SDT autonomy (he
always chooses — the game only asks for the *reason*). Reasons are presence-logged, never scored.

### Delegate or Do? — the specify→delegate→verify curriculum
`engine 03 + 05 (verify)` · `Model B` · bands 12–18 · new — design hypothesis (AI-era flagship)
**Loop.** The Head Ranger band brings helpers (a volunteer NPC; later the in-fiction **assistant**).
For each task: do it yourself (learning value?) or hand it off — and if you hand it off, **write the
brief** (what done looks like — the Definition-of-Done muscle, reused) and **check the work** (the
Fix-the-Machine / Spot-the-Confident-Robot muscle, reused).
**Grows (L10 — this card is the authorship flip, embodied).**
- **12–14:** sort a task list into do/delegate with reasons; delegated work comes back checkably
  right or wrong against his own brief (bad brief → wrong work → the lesson writes itself, kindly).
- **14–16:** multi-step delegation to the assistant with two planted flaws per job; find both before
  approving. Brief quality visibly drives output quality.
- **16–18:** real delegation: using real AI tools for real work (research for school, a draft, a
  plan), with the logged habit — *what did I specify, what did I verify, what stayed mine?* The
  game's role is the debrief, not the tool.
**Varies.** Helpers with different failure styles (literal-minded, overconfident, sloppy-but-fast) —
choosing the *right* helper for the job is the advanced skill.
**Flags.** Design hypothesis — this is the plan's "deciding what to do is the part that stays human"
(PLAN §1) turned into a mechanic. The in-fiction assistant is scripted/local (no cloud needed);
real-AI use at 16–18 is a parent-in-loop graduation lane, not a game feature. Never framed as "AI
replaces work" — framed as *what a good chief does with any helper*.

### The Training Plan — learning to learn, made playable
`engine 03 + 01` · `Model B (+A curves)` · bands 13–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** Pick a skill you actually want (game or real: juggling, guitar riff, free-kicks). Design
the practice: which sub-skill, what edge-of-ability looks like, what feedback source, when. Run it
two weeks; the personal-best curve responds — or doesn't. Adjust the *plan*, not the child.
**Grows (L9, L10).** 13–14 assemble a plan from parts (the deliberate-practice features as cards:
edge, feedback, spacing, variation — doc 03 §6) · 14–16 full self-design + one variable changed at a
time (**CVS on your own practice** — the area-01 seam that turns logic into learning-to-learn) ·
16–18 n=1 experiments ("does morning practice beat evening? does sleep move the curve?" — area-09
seam), and coaching a younger NPC's plan (teach rung).
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; honesty lines from doc 03 §8 apply hard — practice explains a minority
of variance (no 10,000-hours framing), curves are private personal-bests, and the plan is the thing
being debugged, never the boy.

## Cross-area seams (build once)

- **Definition-of-Done → brief-writing** — the same muscle at 8 (checkable done) and at 16 (a spec
  an assistant can't misread). One competency record, two contexts.
- **Predict-Then-Check** is the Sure-O-Meter (area 02) reskinned — doc 03 says don't re-derive it.
- **The Training Plan** joins logic's CVS (01), health's felt experiments (09) and the EF staircase
  into the game's learning-to-learn capstone.
- **The Almanac** is the whole game's portfolio spine (growth model §1); this area owns its shape.
- **Feedback-half mechanics** (stakeholder reacting to a proposal, coach critiquing a finished
  restoration) wait on Engine 04 / PLAN §7.6 and are marked blocked in doc 03 §5.
