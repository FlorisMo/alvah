### Fair Shares — fairness felt before it's argued
`engine 03` · `Model B (reflection artefact)` · bands 8–12 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** Divide something real-feeling among helpers: berries after picking, tools among volunteers,
blame after a mishap. Equal split, effort split, need split — the three collide on purpose (the
smallest helper worked hardest; the biggest is hungriest). Choose, hear how each helper feels, and
say what felt fair.
**Grows (L2).** 8–10 two helpers, one obvious tension · 10–12 three helpers + the quiet one who
doesn't complain (noticing the unsqueaky wheel *is* the lesson) — and division of *chores*, not just
goodies (fair sharing of the unfun).
**Varies.** Goods, work, credit, space (who gets the sunny plot); a "you cut, they choose" mechanic
for the cake-problem classics.
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; fills the §8 young band where culling content is barred. Grounded in
the care/fairness tension (doc 05 §3, 9–10 rung). All three splits are presented as *defensible* —
the artefact is his reasoning, never a verdict.

### The Kept Word — promises have weight
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 9–14 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** A promise made cheaply comes due expensively (promised the kid a fox-watching evening; the
weather's perfect for the heather count). Keep it, renegotiate it honestly, or break it — then sit
with what each costs, including to how the other now trusts you.
**Grows (L5).** 9–11 keep-or-break with visible feelings · 11–14 the third way arrives:
renegotiating *before* breaking (the honest move most kids don't know exists) — and noticing which
promises shouldn't have been made (the prevention lesson).
**Varies.** Promises to people, to animals ("I'll come back and free the snare"), to himself (the
bridge to area 04's commitments).
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; trust-as-repeated-game is folk-solid but unstudied here — keep
consequences honest, not moralised. The world's never-renege rule (doc 02 §1.3) applies to the
*game's* promises; NPCs may break theirs — that asymmetry is the curriculum.

### Who's Affected? — the moral imagination warm-up
`engine 03` · `Model B (stakeholder-map artefact)` · bands 8–18 · seed: 05 §5
**Loop.** Before any decision: map everyone and everything touched — deer, ground-nesters, visitors,
the neighbour, next year's animals.
**Grows (L7).** 8–10 pick affected parties from a picture-set · 10–12 add the *invisible* ones
(the insects under the log; the future) · 12–14 weight arrives: affected *how much*, helped or
harmed? · 14–16 "whose voice is missing?" becomes the standing overlay (feeds The Grant Round and
the Village Meeting) · 16–18 second-order effects (the fence helps lambs, changes deer paths,
shifts the browsing…).
**Varies.** Every other dilemma in this doc opens with this map — it is the area's shared first
move, built once.

### Gut-then-Reasons — intuition acknowledged, then examined
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 9–18 · seed: 05 §5
**Loop.** Log the first gut reaction (it's a feeling, not yet a reason — Haidt's honest order,
doc 05 §3); then two reasons supporting it and one challenging it.
**Grows (L1).** 9–11 gut + one reason · 11–13 the full 2+1 · 13–15 name which *lens* each reason
speaks from (care/consequences/fairness — the pluralist vocabulary arriving) · 15–18 moral-
foundations literacy: recognising loyalty/authority/liberty flavours as *tastes* people differ in
(*waarschijnlijk*, doc 05 §3) — including his own default flavour.
**Flags.** The gut is never wrong *as data*; the practice is noticing it isn't yet an argument.

### The Intervene Dial — the flagship, grown carefully
`engine 03 (+04 pushback)` · `Model B (decision-log artefact)` · bands 12–18 · seed: 05 §5, §7
**Loop.** The slider: do nothing → feed → cull, over a real-shaped rewilding case (the
Oostvaardersplassen pattern). Three legitimate framings (wildness / management / welfare) presented
with equal dignity; outcomes revealed as *plausibly uncertain*, never right/wrong. Step-back option
always on screen; heaviest variants are parent-present.
**Grows (the §8 gates, applied).** 12–13 one animal, one winter, gentle outcomes; the three framings
*named* · 13–15 population scale; he writes each framing's best case before setting the dial
(steel-man built in) · 15–16 a dilemma character who holds the framing he rejected pushes back
(Engine 04, if unblocked) · 16–18 the policy memo: his provisional position, its costs owned, what
would change it (Mind-Changer seam) — revisited a season later by Seasons Replay.
**Varies.** Dial cases rotate: supplementary feeding in a hard winter, the orphaned boar piglet,
predator reintroduction.
**Flags.** Experts themselves disagree — the mechanic *is* that truth (doc 05 §7). Uncertainty in
outcomes is mandatory (never fake clean answers); eco-anxiety guard: agency before bleak facts,
calm-down/normalising step after (doc 05 §8).

### Access vs. Habitat — justice with maps
`engine 03 (+04)` · `Model B (decision brief)` · bands 12–18 · seed: 05 §5
**Loop.** Open or close the trail: visitors' joy and funding vs disturbance and nesting. Competing
*legitimate* voices; produce the decision brief.
**Grows (L8).** 12–14 two voices, one season · 14–16 four voices including economic dependence (the
kiosk lady's livelihood) and the mobility-limited visitor (access is also care) · 16–18 the same
trail, three years running — policy as a *living* decision (feeds the Village Meeting agenda).
**Flags.** Maps to real Natura-2000 tensions (doc 05 §3); no house view.

### The Letter and the Spirit — rules meet judgement
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 13–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** A good rule gives a bad answer here (no fires — but the stranded group is hypothermic; no
off-trail — but the injured dog is off-trail). Follow it, break it, or escalate it — and then the
follow-up that makes it ethics rather than rebellion: *what is the rule protecting, and who should
change it, how?*
**Grows (L7).** 13–15 clear cases (spirit obviously served by the exception) · 15–17 murky ones
(the exception that erodes the rule; "if everyone did this…" — the universalisability move, doc 05
§3's "what would I want as a rule") · 16–18 the repair rung: draft the rule's *improvement* and take
it to the Village Meeting (rules as evolving agreements — the tension→proposal spirit without
governance vocabulary, doc 03 §4).
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; anti-authoritarian *and* anti-cavalier by construction — obedience is
never the virtue (PLAN §5.8 veto), but neither is cleverness against rules; stewardship of the rule
is the mature move.

### Reporting a Neighbour — loyalty vs harm (hard-gated 14+)
`engine 03 (+04)` · `Model B` · bands 14–18 · seed: 05 §5 (doc 05 §8 gates it; parent-present)
**Loop.** The neighbour — a *kind* neighbour, established over years in the cast — is baiting
wildlife. Talk to them first? Report? Look away? Every path costs something real.
**Grows.** 14–16 the direct-conversation path is fully playable (Angry-Farmer skills, for real
stakes) before authority paths unlock · 16–18 variants where talking failed — and where *he* is the
one someone should report (the mirror case, once, carefully).
**Flags.** The heaviest card in the catalogue: parent-present by default, step-back always, no
"correct" ending, tone-checked hardest. The cast history is load-bearing — reporting a stranger
teaches nothing; reporting someone you *like* is the actual dilemma.

### Should This Be Made? — maker ethics for the AI era
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 14–18 · seed: 05 §5
**Loop.** A project has a hidden cost (the drone that films nests disturbs them; the viral post
reveals the owl roost's location). Proceed, modify, or drop — and attribute any AI help honestly.
**Grows (L10).** 14–16 hidden costs discovered mid-project (sunk-cost pressure is part of the
dilemma) · 16–18 the full maker's checklist he writes for himself: who's affected, what's credited,
what would I think seeing this from outside? (Influence Map + provenance habits landing together.)
**Flags.** AI-ethics-for-kids is pre-evidence (doc 05 §9) — frameworks, not validated curricula;
keep it concrete and case-based, never catechism.

### Seasons Replay — the long-consequences mirror
`engine 03` · `Model B (paired artefacts)` · bands 10–18 · seed: 05 §5
**Loop.** A past decision resurfaces a season/year later with its (uncertain) outcome; re-read what
he wrote then, re-reason now. Growth made visible without a grade — the two artefacts side by side.
**Grows (L5).** 10–12 one-season gaps, gentle outcomes · 12–16 year gaps; outcomes sometimes prove
the *other* framing right (being wrong gracefully is the practice) · 16–18 the decade view: his own
ethical through-line, private (the almanac's quietest chapter).
**Flags.** Resurfacing is Engine 03's scheduler doing ethics duty; outcomes stay plausibly-uncertain
— the world never says "see, you were wrong," only "here is what happened."

## Cross-area seams (build once)

- **Who's Affected?** is the shared opening move for the Grant Round (03), Access vs Habitat, the
  Village Meeting (05) and Should This Be Made? — one artefact type, built once.
- **Steel-manning** is the 02/05/08 shared record; the Intervene Dial builds it in at 13+.
- **Gut-then-Reasons** pairs naturally with the Body Weather Vane (06): the gut is *felt* before
  it's labelled — the interoception seam giving ethics its body.
- **The Letter and the Spirit → Village Meeting**: rule-improvement proposals become agendas.
- **Ethics never scores**: every card writes Engine-03 variant-B-adjacent artefacts (reflection,
  never rated); AI characters may *push back* but never evaluate (doc 06 §12c).
