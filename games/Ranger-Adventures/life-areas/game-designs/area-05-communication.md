### The Angry Farmer — say the true thing under heat
`engine 03 (+04 when unblocked)` · `Model B, presence-only` · bands 8–18 · seed: 04 §5
**Loop.** The farmer is upset (the fence, the dog, the wolf rumour). Answer in a way that names
fact + feeling + need + ask instead of blame. The note records only *that* a non-blaming line was
tried.
**Grows (L1, L4, L8).** 8–10 pick from three lines (blame / half-way / clean), hear them aloud —
the *sound* of blame vs clean is the curriculum · 10–12 assemble the four NVC pieces from tiles
(icons carry the structure, doc 04 §10) · 12–14 **speak it freeform**; the farmer reacts to what was
actually said (Engine 04, lower tier — this is the seed's true form) · 14–16 heat rises: the farmer
interrupts, exaggerates, is a bit unfair — hold the line without escalating; anger = unmet need,
heard live · 16–18 **the real-conversation lane**: prep a real hard talk (rehearse against a
persona), have it in real life, debrief privately. Real-people content is highest-sensitivity:
on-device or parent-mediated only, never cloud.
**Varies.** The cast carries it: farmer (fear for lambs), hotel owner (money worry), the farmer's
kid (embarrassment) — same move, different need behind the anger.
**Flags.** NVC evidence is thin (doc 04 §9) — the game says "this often helps," never "this works."
Parent co-play modelling beats solo reps at 8–10 (co-regulation, doc 04 §7).

### Listening Lantern — make the other person feel got
`engine 03 (+04)` · `Model B, presence-only` · bands 8–18 · seed: 04 §5
**Loop.** An NPC carries a worry; the real conversation only unlocks after a paraphrase-and-check
("so you're worried that… did I get it?"). The lantern lights when the *paraphrase step happened*.
**Grows (L4, L8).** 8–10 pick the fair paraphrase from three · 10–12 build it (feeling + content
tiles) · 12–14 freeform paraphrase, NPC confirms/corrects ("no — it's not the money, it's the
lambs") — the correction *is* the game · 14–16 listen past a messy, rambling telling; find the need
under it · 16–18 double duty: paraphrase *before* disagreeing in every hard scene (the habit
generalises across the whole game — a global toggle, not a mini-game).
**Varies.** Worried visitor, sad kid who lost a dog, excited-but-confusing scientist (listening to
enthusiasm is also listening).
**Flags.** Reflective listening has modest *real* evidence (doc 04 §4) — the area's safest bet;
lead with it.

### Repair the Rope Bridge — after the rupture
`engine 03 (+04)` · `Model B, presence-only ("repair attempted")` · bands 10–18 · seed: 04 §5
**Loop.** A falling-out with an ally (a snapped word, a broken promise, a misunderstanding). Days
later, the bridge to their side is out. Re-open, own your part, restore.
**Grows (L5, L8).** 10–12 guided repair: three beats (name it, own your bit, ask to rebuild) with
tiles · 12–14 the other side is still hurt and says so; stay in it without re-litigating · 14–16
repairs where *both* were partly right (most real ones) — own your part without swallowing theirs ·
16–18 time-delayed repairs (an old rupture resurfaces — it's never too late) and, privately, real
ones debriefed (highest sensitivity, on-device only).
**Varies.** Rupture types rotate: broken promise, public embarrassment, taking credit, the
misunderstanding nobody caused.
**Flags.** Conflict-repair is well-regarded practice with thin formal evidence (doc 04 §3) — frame
as craft, not cure. The U-Turn Gate (area 06) is this game's inner half: check your own part first.

### The Right Question — getting the real information out
`engine 03 (+04)` · `Model B` · bands 8–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** A mystery only questions can solve (the lost dog, the sick oak, what the visitor actually
needs). A question budget makes quality felt: open beats closed, one-at-a-time beats stacked,
"what happened just before?" beats "why did you do that?"
**Grows (L4, L10).** 8–10 pick the better of two questions; watch what each unlocks · 10–12 author
questions; count how many the answer needed · 12–14 interview a *reluctant* teller (short answers
until the question shows real interest) · 14–16 diagnose with questions under time (triage the
storm damage by asking the right three people the right three things) · 16–18 **asking machines**:
write the question/brief that gets a useful answer from the in-fiction assistant on the first try —
the same craft, honestly named (Delegate-or-Do seam, area 04).
**Varies.** Question-styles as collectible field-guide pages (the widener, the narrower, the
time-walker, the feeling-finder) — met through use, named after.
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; grounded in active-listening/perspective-taking lineage (doc 04 §4)
plus the epistemics "how do you know?" habit (doc 01 §3). No interrogation framing — questions serve
the *other person's* telling.

### The Polite No — boundaries without blame
`engine 03 (+04)` · `Model B, presence-only` · bands 10–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** Visitors ask for things the ranger can't allow (a campfire in a dry week, a shortcut
through the nesting field, "just one selfie with the fawn"). Decline clearly, name the reason,
offer what *is* possible. Also the mirror: the ranger must *accept* a no from others gracefully
(request vs demand — the NVC rung, doc 04 §3).
**Grows (L8).** 10–12 assemble the no (no + because + alternative) · 12–14 the asker pushes back
twice; hold it kindly without new reasons (the broken-record skill) · 14–16 saying no to someone
with *power* in the fiction (the mayor, the big donor) — and to a friend · 16–18 the real-life lane,
privately debriefed: one real no that mattered.
**Varies.** Cheerful chancers, genuinely needy cases (a no that hurts), and requests that should be
a *yes* (discernment, not reflex).
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; the request-vs-demand distinction is core NVC (doc 04 §3, 11–12 rung).
Accepting-a-no rounds guard against the skill curdling into stonewalling.

### Teach the New Volunteer — explaining as the test of knowing
`engine 03` · `Model B (portfolio: the lesson artefact)` · bands 8–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** A new volunteer must learn something the ranger has mastered (a knot, a call, the feeding
round). Explain it; the volunteer then *does exactly what was said* — literal-minded execution makes
every gap in the explanation visible and funny, never shaming.
**Grows (L7, L10 — this is the teach rung of the authorship flip).** 8–10 order the picture-steps,
volunteer follows · 10–12 record spoken instructions; watch them followed to the letter (the missing
step = the comedy = the lesson) · 12–14 teach a *judgement* skill, not just a procedure ("how do you
know the ice is safe?") — rules of thumb must be articulated · 14–16 debug the volunteer's
misunderstanding (find *which* sentence planted it — Fix-the-Machine seam) · 16–18 design a tiny
curriculum: three lessons in the right order for next year's volunteer (and, in real life, actually
teach someone something, debriefed).
**Varies.** Volunteer personalities (eager-literal, distracted, over-confident) change what good
teaching needs; content comes from *his own* mastered skills across all areas — the almanac
suggests candidates.
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; the protégé-effect framing is plausible-not-proven — the honest claim
is doc 06 §8's: explaining is *evidence of* understanding, and a lovely portfolio artefact.

### The Village Meeting — many voices, one next step
`engine 04 + 03` · `Model B` · bands 15–18 · new — design hypothesis · **blocked on PLAN §7.6**
**Loop.** A genuinely contested reserve issue (the trail closure, the wolf protocol) with three-four
NPC voices that want different things. The ranger facilitates: everyone heard (Listening Lantern, at
scale), the disagreement mapped (When-Experts-Disagree seam), and *one agreed next step* found —
not consensus on everything, just the next step.
**Grows (L8).** 15–16 two parties + the mentor co-chairs · 16–18 four parties, one of them loud,
one silent (drawing out the quiet voice is the hidden objective); he chairs alone; the decision
brief goes in the almanac.
**Varies.** Issues rotate through the ethics catalogue (area 08 dilemmas make natural agendas).
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; multi-party generative dialogue is the most Engine-04-demanding thing
in the whole plan — build last, after single-persona scenes prove out. Facilitation-not-winning is
the tension→action spirit without governance vocabulary (doc 03 §4 veto).
