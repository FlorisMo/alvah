### Ten-Monster Sprint — fluency before judgement
`engine 03` · `Model B (portfolio)` · bands 8–18 · seed: 05 §5
**Loop.** Fill the page: ten monsters (or huts, flags, traps, tunes) fast, no judging — judge-mode
is locked out by design during the sprint.
**Grows (L3, L10).** 8–10 pure quantity, big paper energy · 10–12 sprint + one keeper tagged after
(the divergent→convergent hinge) · 12–14 constrained sprints ("ten monsters that live underwater")
— constraints arrive exactly when the §9 dip (~12–13) makes blank pages scary · 14–16 cross-media
sprints (ten in clay, sound, words) — domain-specificity honoured by *visiting* domains · 16–18
sprints in service of real briefs (ten openings for *his* film; ten names for the kiosk product).
**Varies.** Sprint seeds from the reserve (ten uses for storm-fallen wood); parent-vs-kid parallel
sprints (co-play, both pages in the almanac).
**Flags.** Fluency training builds habit, not measured "creativity" (doc 05 §3) — the sheet count is
presence, never a score.

### The Keeper Pick — choosing is a skill
`engine 03 + 05` · `Model B` · bands 8–18 · seed: 05 §5
**Loop.** From a sprint's output, pick one to develop and say why against a small number of concrete
criteria. The set *and* the reasoning go in the portfolio.
**Grows (L1).** 8–10 pick the favourite, one spoken reason · 10–12 two concrete criteria ("scariest"
+ "buildable") · 12–14 criteria he writes first, then picks against them (pre-commitment — the
epistemics move in maker clothes) · 14–16 defend a keeper the criteria *don't* favour ("the rules
say A, my gut says B — here's why B") — taste finding its voice · 16–18 kill-your-darlings rounds:
choosing what *not* to develop, kindly.
**Flags.** Convergent thinking emerges later than divergent (doc 05 §3) — keep criteria few and
concrete young.

### Three-Block Build + Constraint Season Packs — limits that free
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 9–18 · seed: 05 §5 (packs: new variation system)
**Loop.** Build something expressive under a hard material limit (three blocks, two colours, one
sound). The pack system rotates the constraint each season so the game tunes toward his inverted-U
sweet spot (doc 05 §1.3).
**Grows (L4).** 9–11 given constraint, free goal · 11–13 given goal, chosen constraint ("pick your
limit, then build") · 13–15 constraint *stacking* (three blocks AND silent AND for-the-dark) ·
15–18 self-imposed constraints as style ("I only work in found wood this season" — constraint
becomes voice).
**Varies.** Seasonal packs as data: winter = monochrome pack, spring = sound pack, storm week =
broken-materials pack. Tighten/loosen per observed frustration (the Goldilocks tuning is the
parent-facing knob).
**Flags.** Constraints help *within* the inverted-U (*zeker*, doc 05 §1.3) — too many stifle; the
pack system exists to tune, not to escalate forever.

### Ugly First Draft — iteration as identity
`engine 03` · `Model B (version history)` · bands 10–18 · seed: 05 §5
**Loop.** v1 is deliberately rough — the game *requires* ugliness (a crayon-only first pass, a
30-second hum). Then v2, v3, one named change per pass; the version strip shows the arc.
**Grows (L5).** 10–12 three passes, game names the change candidates · 12–14 he names the change
before each pass ("this pass is only about the roofline") · 14–16 longer arcs (v5+), including a
pass that makes it *worse* and gets reverted — dead ends normalised · 16–18 draft culture as
default: everything he makes anywhere gets a v1 that's allowed to be bad.
**Flags.** The version strip is the anti-perfectionism machine — protect it from any polish-first
framing; the ~12–13 dip is exactly when v1-shame arrives (doc 05 §3), so this game front-loads there.

### Voice Statement — original intent
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 12–18 · seed: 05 §5
**Loop.** Before making: one line — "this is trying to ___." Revisit it mid-way and at the end: did
the thing drift, and was the drift good?
**Grows (L10).** 12–14 pick the intent from options, notice drift at the end · 14–16 write it; the
mid-way check becomes the interesting beat (keep the plan or follow the discovery — both are wins if
*chosen*) · 16–18 intent across works: what do five statements have in common? (feeds The Gallery.)
**Flags.** Creative identity consolidates in adolescence — support, don't impose (doc 05 §3).

### Critique Cards — the taste flagship
`engine 05` · `Model B` · bands 10–18 · seed: 05 §5–§6
**Loop.** Judge-mode room: a piece (never his own, at first) + the small shared rubric
(intent/craft/surprise) + always one strength and one what-if. Process language only.
**Grows (the engine-05 mode ladder is the growth).** 10–12 judge-external, far-apart pairs · 12–14
near-ties; exemplars from real craft traditions · 14–16 judge-AI (Spot the AI Slop merges here);
self-critique begins on *old* work (six-months-ago him is emotionally "someone else" — the safe
bridge to self-critique) · 16–18 live critique exchange with the critique-partner persona (Engine
04, if unblocked), and of current work.
**Varies.** Rubric dimensions stay fixed (one muscle — engine 05 §10 warns against sprawl); the
*artefact domain* rotates: posters, birdhouses, trail layouts, story openings, melodies.
**Flags.** What aesthetic-education actually improves is *consistency of judgement* (doc 05 §1.2) —
the rising-standards portfolio shows exactly that, never a talent verdict.

### Spot the AI Slop — taste against the flood
`engine 05 (+04 for live foils)` · `Model B` · bands 12–18 · seed: 05 §5
**Loop.** Three AI options for a real need (the reserve's poster, sign copy, a jingle); pick which
serves the intent, say why the others fail; older bands repair the best one.
**Grows (L2, L10).** 12–14 one option is obviously hollow (generic, wrong audience) — name *why* it
fails though it's "pretty" · 14–16 near-ties + repair ("keep the layout, gut the slogan") · 16–18
the full workflow: brief → generate → curate → repair → ship, with his name on the *choices*
(Art Director seam).
**Varies.** Slop failure-modes rotate like Confident-Robot error types: generic-pretty,
off-intent, over-decorated, uncanny, plagiarism-adjacent (feeds Influence Map's attribution ethic).
**Flags.** Curated foils need no LLM (buildable now); live generation waits on §7.6. "Detecting AI"
is *not* the skill (unprovable, doc 01 §7) — **serving the intent** is; an AI option that genuinely
serves it can *win*, which keeps the game honest.

### Make It For Someone — audience enters the studio
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 8–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** A gift brief: make a thing for a *specific* someone (the mentor who loves owls; the
farmer's kid who's scared of the dark; a real person, with parent). First: what do they love, fear,
need? (Two Right-Questions, area 05.) Then make. Then — the beat that matters — *watch them meet
it* and log one observation.
**Grows (L8, L9).** 8–10 in-fiction recipients with visible loves · 10–12 the observe-and-revise
beat arrives (v2 after seeing their face — user testing without the jargon) · 12–14 harder
recipients (someone he disagrees with; someone very unlike him) · 14–16 real recipients, real
gifts; the log stays private · 16–18 an *audience of many*: the visitor-centre wall piece — one
thing that must land for kids *and* grandparents (the pro brief).
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; grounded in doc 05's authentic-assessment/studio rationale and the
perspective-taking arc (doc 04 §3). Empathy is the input, never the graded output.

### The Influence Map — where ideas come from
`engine 03 + 05` · `Model B` · bands 13–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** Take a work he loves. Name two *moves* it makes (not "it's cool" — "the dark edge makes
the middle glow"). Use one move, visibly, in his own next piece — and credit it on the piece's
almanac page ("the glow move is from ___").
**Grows (L10).** 13–15 find the move, borrow it, credit it · 15–17 trace a move through three works
(his influences have influences) · 16–18 the flip: find *his* moves — what would someone borrow
from him? (feeds The Gallery's through-line.)
**Flags.** Design hypothesis. This is the attribution ethic made native *before* the AI question
makes it fraught: influence-with-credit vs copying — the exact line "Should This Be Made?" (area
08) needs at 14+. No surnames in-game: credit works and roles, not named artists (CLAUDE.md rule 1).

### Art Director — directing the machine
`engine 04 + 05` · `Model B` · bands 14–18 · new — design hypothesis · **blocked on PLAN §7.6**
**Loop.** He holds the intent; the machine generates. Three rounds: brief → options → redirect
("warmer, less crowded, the owl bigger") → converge or overrule (make the final call by hand if the
machine can't get there). The portfolio keeps the *brief evolution* — the visible artefact of taste
directing generation.
**Grows (L10 — the specify-&-verify rung, creative form).** 14–16 redirect vocabulary grows from
tiles to free direction; learn when to stop asking and start making · 16–18 full pipeline ownership
on real briefs (the kiosk poster, the expedition film titles), including the ethics beat: what gets
credited, what stays his (Should-This-Be-Made seam).
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; lower-sensitivity (about a made thing) so cloud-eligible on opt-in
*only* (doc 06 §12a). The design must keep hand-making prestigious — the machine is one tool on the
bench, and briefs that end in "I made it myself instead" are logged as wins too.

### The Gallery — curation closes the decade
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 15–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** From ten years of portfolio: choose five works, sequence them, write the through-line
("what was I always trying to do?"). Hang them in the in-fiction visitor centre — and, at 18, maybe
on a real wall.
**Grows.** 15–16 a season's gallery (small, practice) · 16–18 the decade gallery; the almanac's
final chapter and the graduation artefact.
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; this is doc 05 §3's 18–21 "portfolio curation & standards" rung pulled
into reach. Self-direction scaffolding, never a judgement of worth.

## Cross-area seams (build once)

- **Judge-mode is one room:** Critique Cards, Spot the AI Slop and Spot the Confident Robot (02) are
  one Engine-05 muscle over different artefacts (doc 05 §6 says so; resist rubric sprawl).
- **The Kiosk (03)** is the standing real-brief supplier: make → price → sell → should-we?
- **Influence Map ↔ ethics:** attribution practice feeds Should This Be Made? (08) and the
  provenance habit (02).
- **Make It For Someone ↔ communication:** the recipient interview is The Right Question, applied.
- **The studio rooms are diegetic:** make-mode = the workshop; judge-mode = the visitor-centre
  review table. Walking between rooms *is* the mode switch (younger bands learn the separation with
  their feet).
