### The foundations cluster (8–12)
**Wants-vs-Needs Sorter** `01·A` — drag items into bins under a budget cap; descriptive feedback,
never moralised. *Grows:* obvious pairs → near-ties ("new boots when yours pinch — want or need?")
→ 11+ the honest wrinkle: the same item flips category with context.
**The Goal Jar** `03·B` — run a saving goal to completion; the jar fills visibly; zero randomness.
*Grows (L5):* days → weeks → a season; one jar → the four-jar split (Save/Spend/Give/Invest, doc 02
§2) with his own chosen ratios at 11+.
**The Reliable World** `03·B` — now-vs-later quests where later *always* pays as promised; the
curriculum is waiting *strategies* (distraction, pre-commitment, out-of-sight), not willpower.
*Grows:* longer horizons, self-invented strategies taught back to a younger NPC (authorship flip).
*Flag:* the never-renege rule is load-bearing (doc 02 §1.3); no exceptions, ever, anywhere in the game.

### Run-a-Budget — the recurring spine
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 10–17 · seed: 02 §4
**Loop.** Cover the reserve's fixed costs, allocate what's left, survive the period; the ledger is
visible and manipulable (drag coins, not type numbers — doc 02 §9).
**Grows (L5, L6, L9).** 10–12 one week, three categories · 12–14 a month, a surprise expense, the
first buffer ("the winter pot") · 14–16 income volatility (grant cycles, good/bad visitor seasons) +
a savings target + a Give allocation (feeds The Grant Round) · 16–17 the budget quietly becomes a
**mirror of his real money** (Graduation Ledger lane).
**Varies.** Reserve budget, expedition budget, kiosk budget, (later) his own real month.
**Flags.** Behaviour-transfer is weak (doc 02 §8) — the recurring *money conversation* with the
parent is the active ingredient; the game schedules it, gently.

### Percent Forge — the gate, honoured
`engine 01` · `Model A` · bands 11–14 · seed: 02 §4
**Loop.** "X% of Y" in money contexts, staircased; discounts → interest → rates of change.
**Grows.** The staircase does the work; the *gate* is the design: Compounding Garden, Transparent-
Odds Choice and Fee Leak stay locked until this is fluent — the #1 too-early trap (doc 02 §1.1) is
teaching compounding to a child who will encode it linearly.
**Varies.** Kiosk discounts, grant percentages, "the boar population grew 20%."
**Flags.** Feeds from the Fact Dojo's fraction↔percent deck (area 01). No timed pressure (doc 02 §9).

### The growth-and-risk cluster (12–16, post-gate)
**Doubling Machine** `02·A` — predict value after N doublings, then the reveal; the gasp *is* the
lesson (exponential-bias inoculation). *Grows:* grains-on-chessboard → pond weed covering the pond →
backwards mode ("how many doublings until…?") → 16+ negative doublings (decay, depreciation).
**Compounding Garden** `03·B` — a tree grows each season on the *new* total; fees are pruning
shears. *Grows:* watch it → set the contribution rhythm → compare two gardens (early-small vs
late-big — the time-in-market felt lesson) → link to the real ledger.
**Transparent-Odds Choice** `02·A` — posted odds, reason-before-act, EV revealed over capped
attempts. *Grows (L1):* single outcome → multi-outcome → variance shown as spread-of-endings →
"which would you regret?" reflection beat (bridges to ethics of risk).
**Ruin Island** `02·A` — repeated favourable bets, but a wipe-out ends the run; fractional sizing is
the way off the island. *Grows:* fixed fractions offered → he sets the fraction → 16+ the Kelly
*intuition* in words ("a good bet can still ruin you if you bet too much of what you have," doc 02
§5 — never the formula).
**Flags (cluster).** This cluster is where the anti-gambling bright line lives daily: odds always
visible, attempts capped, no near-miss theatre, no escalation. Ruin Island exists to teach that
reckless EV-chasing *loses* — explicitly anti-gambling (doc 02 §4).

### The investing cluster (14–18)
**The Fee Leak** `02·A` — same portfolio at 0.1% vs 1.5% over decades; the gap drawn, felt, named
(fees = compounding in reverse). **Inflation Tides** `03·B` — the still jar quietly shrinks in
buying power; the moving jar doesn't. **Index vs Picker** `02·A+B` — hold-the-market vs
pick-and-trade over simulated decades; rewards holding, never trading thrills (modelled returns, no
slot feel). **Diversify the Harvest** `03·B` — plant one crop vs five; a bad year hits differently
(framed as lowering downside, doc 02 §4).
*Grows (cluster):* each starts as a parable (12–14 can watch), becomes a puzzle with numbers
(14–16), then reads as a checklist against the real ledger (16–18: "what's the fee on *our* fund?").
*Flags:* index-first is the *zeker* stance (doc 02 §3); never stock-picking-as-skill framing.

### Ranger's Kiosk — earning: where value comes from
`engine 03` · `Model B (portfolio)` · bands 8–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** Run the visitor kiosk: choose stock, set prices, watch demand respond, count the till.
Value = what someone will gladly trade for; earning = making something people value.
**Grows (L4, L9, L10).** 8–10 helper at the stand: pick the price from three, see what happens ·
10–12 stock choices with unsold-leftovers lesson (waste = cost) · 12–14 cost vs margin; a rainy-week
cash cushion (budget seam) · 14–16 a small venture end-to-end: make (area 07), price, sell at the
in-fiction fair; elasticity felt ("halve the price, triple the buyers?") · 16–18 **the real thing**,
small: sell something real (market stall, online, a service), the game only logs and debriefs
(rung 4).
**Varies.** Kiosk ↔ plant nursery ↔ guided-walk tickets; a "what would *you* pay?" visitor-interview
beat (communication seam).
**Flags.** Design hypothesis — doc 02's spiral covers spend/save/invest but not earning; grounded in
its experiential-economy evidence (§3, classroom-economy RCT) and §7's real-stakes lever. No
get-rich framing (doc 02 §3 veto); the kiosk funds the *reserve*, tying earning to purpose.

### The Grant Round — allocating with reasons
`engine 03` · `Model B (reflection artefact)` · bands 12–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** A fixed pot; competing reserve projects apply (new hide, frog ladder, school visits).
Allocate, and write the one-line reason each applicant hears.
**Grows (L8, L10).** 12–14 three applicants, clear differences · 14–16 near-ties + a "who benefits,
who's missing?" overlay (ethics seam — the Give jar grows up into philanthropy-with-judgement) ·
16–18 run the round *as chair* with NPC panellists who disagree (Engine 04, if unblocked); publish
the decision brief (almanac artefact).
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; EV language meets values language on purpose — the reasons are the
curriculum, and they are never scored (no morality score, doc 05).

### Fair Trade at the Market — negotiation without manipulation
`engine 03 (+04 later)` · `Model B` · bands 9–16 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** Trade tools, seeds and favours with villagers. Both sides have needs; a good trade names
them. Anchors: opportunity cost ("this hammer is my ladder-money") and the walk-away option.
**Grows (L8).** 9–11 barter with visible want-lists · 12–14 the other side's constraint is hidden —
*ask* before offering (The Right Question seam, area 05) · 14–16 three-way trades; reputation: the
market remembers fair dealers (trust made mechanical, gently).
**Flags.** Design hypothesis. Hard boundary from doc 04's veto: no scripted-manipulation/"persuasion
technique" framing — the skill is finding the trade both sides *actually* want, out loud.

### Graduation Ledger — the handoff instrument
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 13–18 · seed: 02 §4, playbook doc 02 §7
**Loop.** A ledger mirroring a real account (savings, then the co-managed index fund); every real
decision gets a logged reason; parent co-signs (Dutch specifics: doc 02 §7 — *machtiging*, Box 3,
the hard 18th-birthday handover).
**Grows (L9).** 13–14 real savings visible in the ledger · 14–16 the co-managed fund with pre-commit
rules ("we don't sell on a scary week") · 16–18 dry-run full control; at 18 the account is legally
his and the ledger becomes the almanac's money chapter.
**Flags.** Readiness signals before real stakes (doc 02 §7): Percent Forge cleared, chooses lower
variance under ruin *and says why*, behaviour shows up outside the game. No cloud, no chance,
parent-in-loop by design.
