### The discrimination family — one staircase, many senses
`engine 01 (3-down/1-up, 79.4%)` · `Model A` · seeds: 07 §5–6
The Model-A skins are one machine with different stimulus knobs (doc 07 §6); their growth is the
engine's knobs turned in a shared order, so it is given once:

**Grows (L2, L3, all skins).**
- **8–10 · coarse + joyful:** far-apart pairs (koolmees vs buizerd; fox vs deer print), long looks,
  2AFC, generous wins. The pattern *library* is small and loved.
- **10–12 · the library grows:** one-new-thing-a-week cadence (one bird call, one track, one bark
  texture), spaced re-exposure by Engine 03's scheduler; 3-choice trials arrive.
- **12–14 · fine discrimination:** same-genus confusables, shorter clips, partial prints; the
  staircase starts genuinely staircasing (JND territory for pitch/texture).
- **14–16 · noise + speed:** wind on the mic, dusk light, brief exposures, layered scenes (the
  expertise model: chunks under pressure, doc 07 §1.2).
- **16–18 · chosen depth:** he picks one or two domains to push toward genuine expertise (all
  warblers by song; every Veluwe track aged); the rest stay maintenance-level. Depth is *his* call
  (authorship flip) — the honest shape of perceptual skill (narrow wins, chosen).

**Per-skin notes.**
- **Name That Call** — the anchor skin; seasonal libraries are the variety engine (spring dawn
  chorus vs autumn contact calls). *Never a reading claim* (doc 07 §4).
- **Pitch Path** — the pure-JND flagship (doc 07 §6); QUEST upgrade candidate if trials must be few.
- **Rhythm of the Forest** — beat-copy on woodpecker drums and cuckoo cadence; the one audio skin
  with *possible* phonology spillover — kept fun-first, remediation-framing barred (doc 07 §4).
- **What Changed Since Yesterday** — change detection over his patch; grows from prop-moved to
  season-crept (the phenology bridge).
- **Spot the Hidden Animal** — camouflage-breaking; exposure time is the knob; 16+ eye-path
  efficiency ("where did you look first?" as curiosity, not score).
- **Read the Track** — ID half here; the field-log half is Model B (the §5 clean split); 14+ adds
  *ageing* the sign (fresh vs old) — inference stacked on discrimination.
- **Match the Texture** — bark/moss/sand by sight then by touch (mystery-bag co-play); toward the
  finger-size floor in the teens (doc 07 §3c).
- **Ranger's Nose / Kitchen Patrol** — the real-world, parent-assisted pair (scent set of the week;
  taste-wheel suppers). Blind ladders are the teen A-side; the diaries stay B. Sapere-grounded
  (RCT-grade for acceptance, doc 07 §4) — and entirely alcohol-free by design, coffee-cupping model
  never wine (doc 07 §3c).
- **Spectrogram Detective** — teen cross-coding (call ↔ picture-of-sound); the bridge kids who love
  charts deserve.

### Slow-Look Journal — the awareness flagship
`engine 03` · `Model B (portfolio, never threshold-scored)` · bands 8–18 · seed: 07 §5, §7
**Loop.** Thirty seconds of just looking; then the universal capture verb — *I notice / I wonder /
it reminds me of* — by voice, drawing or photo. The journal is the artefact; richness made visible,
never ranked (doc 07 §7).
**Grows (L4, L5).** 8–10 one notice per entry, any sense · 10–12 the three verbs used apart on
purpose; vocabulary cloud grows (his words) · 12–14 longer holds; drawing-as-seeing enters (the
Edwards exercises *minus* the debunked brain talk, doc 07 §4) · 14–16 comparative entries (same oak,
four seasons; same dune, wet and dry) · 16–18 a self-directed field study (doc 07 §3e's 15–21 rung)
— his question, his protocol (CVS seam), a season of entries, a written-up almanac chapter.
**Varies.** Sense-of-the-week prompts; micro-subjects (one square meter) vs vistas; night entries.
**Flags.** The §7 rules are law: portfolio never threshold, cadence over intensity, wonder is the
reward. The John-Muir-Laws role-model frame (dyslexic naturalist; difference as a way of seeing) is
the area's quiet gift to this specific boy — use it.

### The Sit-Spot — baseline, belonging, and bird language
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 8–18 · seed: 07 §5
**Loop.** One chosen spot, returned to across years. Log who's about and the mood of the birds;
over time the *baseline* becomes readable — and a broken baseline (sudden silence) becomes
information.
**Grows (L5, L8).** 8–10 co-play visits, "how many sounds?" · 10–12 the regulars get names; first
baseline feel · 12–14 alarm-vs-song distinctions; predict the unseen (cat? hawk?) from the birds'
behaviour — perception becoming inference · 14–16 seasonal baseline shifts; the spot's year graphed
in his journal · 16–18 the spot is simply *his* — the practice has dissolved into a relationship
(the graduation this area wants; mentoring a younger kid at their first sit-spot is the teach rung).
**Flags.** Bird-language reading is tradition-strong, evidence-thin (doc 07 §4 contemplative tier)
— labelled so, portfolio-only.

### Phenology Wheel — the year made visible
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 8–18 · seed: 07 §5
**Loop.** Log firsts (first frog, first heather bloom, first frost, first returning migrant) on a
circular year-wheel; each year adds a ring.
**Grows (L5).** 8–10 big firsts, parent-prompted · 10–12 his own watch-list · 12–16 multi-year
comparison ("the heather is early this year — why might that be?" — epistemics seam) · 16–18 his
wheel vs regional records (real citizen-science lane, parent-checked — rung 4).
**Flags.** The wheel is the almanac's most beautiful page and the game's calendar heartbeat
(growth-model §4); collection mechanics only, no completionism pressure (missing a first is
weather, not failure).

### Body Weather Report — interoception, perception side only
`engine 03` · `Model B (private)` · bands 8–18 · seed: 07 §5
**Loop.** After movement: where do you feel breath, heartbeat, warmth? Locate, describe, rate
faint→strong. **Notice & describe = here; interpret & change state = area 06** (the doc 07 §8
boundary, kept clean).
**Grows (L1).** 8–10 Jumping-Jack-style locating, playful · 10–13 sensation vocabulary widens;
before/after contrasts · 13–18 subtler and blended signals described in his own words — resolution,
not meaning.
**Flags.** Never a heartbeat-accuracy score (validity contested, doc 07 §4); private,
presence-only, like all interoception.

### Zoom & Wide — attention as a lens you choose
`engine 03 (+01 pairs)` · `Model B` · bands 10–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** The Deep-Listening toggle made playable (doc 07 §7.4): on cue, zoom to one sound and
follow only it; on cue, open wide and count the whole field. The skill is *switching on purpose*,
not holding forever.
**Grows (L3).** 10–12 long cues, obvious sounds · 12–14 faster switches; zoom targets that move
(follow the wren through the scene) · 14–18 visual and interoceptive variants (zoom to one leaf /
one body signal, then wide) — one attentional gesture across senses.
**Varies.** Soundscape recordings by season; live outdoor rounds (co-play, eyes closed).
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; it operationalises a doc 07 §7 principle that had no seed. Rides
Attention Campfire's honesty line: the score, where any, is the round's, not the child's.

### Read the Sky — sensing meets forecasting
`engine 02` · `Model A (delayed reveal)` · bands 11–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** From felt and seen signs — cloud types, wind shift, pressure feel, swallows flying low —
predict tomorrow's weather, with confidence (Sure-O-Meter layer). Tomorrow tells the truth.
**Grows (L1, L5).** 11–13 rain-or-not with two signs · 13–15 multi-sign readings; calibration curve
per season · 15–18 storm-timing and microclimates ("the heath dries faster than the wood"); his
forecast vs the official one — where does the app beat senses, and where do senses win locally?
(The honest AI-era punchline: instruments are strong, *local embodied priors* still add value —
and knowing which is which is epistemics.)
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; composes two existing engines (02's delayed reveal + this area's
stimuli), no new machinery. Never frames app-checking as cheating — cross-checking *is* the skill.
**The diegetic jewel:** weather-reading is the most ranger of all ranger skills and it makes
Prediction Diary concrete daily.

### The Map in Your Head — navigation without the blue dot
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 9–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** Walk a route in the fiction (later: a real one, co-play), then draw the map from memory —
landmarks, turns, sounds, smells. Compare to the real map *afterwards*; the deltas are the
curriculum ("I shrank the boring stretch").
**Grows (L4, L9).** 9–11 three-landmark routes, big features · 11–13 sketch-maps with distances
felt in minutes; navigate *back* by landmarks alone · 13–15 route-planning from his own map;
sun/moss/church-tower orientation tricks · 15–18 real hikes navigated GPS-last (device sealed for
emergencies), then the honest debrief: what did the head-map get wrong, and what did it *know* that
the app map doesn't (the muddy stretch, the wind tunnel)?
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; spatial-representation practice with the same narrow-transfer honesty
as everything here (a better mental map of the Veluwe, not "better spatial IQ"). Real-world rounds
are parent-in-loop with the safety override always on.

## Cross-area seams (build once)

- **The staircase family is the architecture-thesis showcase** — every Model-A skin here is a
  stimulus library on engine 01 (doc 07's reuse win; the recommended first vertical slice).
- **Slow-Look / Sit-Spot / Phenology are the almanac's sensory chapters**; the capture verb
  ("I notice / I wonder / it reminds me of") is Engine 03's shared front door with area 04's
  Notice-and-Name — built once (engine 03 §10.5).
- **Read the Sky** wires this area into calibration (02) and the Prediction Diary.
- **Read the Track's inference rung** feeds Cause Detective and Evidence Tipper (01) — perception
  handing evidence to reasoning is the game's oldest loop ("is this really a wolf's track?").
- **Body Weather Report ↔ area 06**: notice-and-describe here, interpret-and-regulate there;
  **Kitchen Patrol / Ranger's Nose ↔ area 09**: the same suppers, fuel-flavoured there,
  flavour-flavoured here. Co-play by nature (doc 07 §12).
