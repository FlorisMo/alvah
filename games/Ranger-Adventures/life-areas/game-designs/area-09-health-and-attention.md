>
> **The design law that makes this area safe to build at all:**
> **the avatar carries the consequences; the child carries only the noticing.** The *ranger* has
> energy, sleep and fuel, and his day visibly runs on them — that is how the model (substrate →
> capability) gets taught. The game **never** reads, asks for, or scores Alvah's own sleep, food,
> weight or body. The real-life layer is optional, private, felt-experiment-shaped reflection —
> presence-only, per the doc 04 hard lines (no weight/calorie anything, no shame, no surveillance).

### The Ranger's Day — the avatar energy loop (the area's spine)
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 8–18 · new — design hypothesis (Fuel Garden grown into a system)
**Loop.** The ranger's capability today runs on last night and this morning: rested + fed → steady
hands on the nest repair, sharp eyes on the far ridge; skipped rest → fumbles, missed calls, a
shorter day. The player *manages the ranger's* rest, meals and pacing as part of playing — and the
consequences are visible, gentle, and recoverable (a tired ranger isn't punished; he just needs a
sit-down and a sandwich, and the day resumes).
**Grows (L7, L10).** 8–10 binary and cheerful: bedtime and breakfast before the big trek, effects
felt same-day · 10–12 pacing arrives — hard tasks cost more; rest *before* the storm beats rest
after · 12–14 the pattern layer: the ranger's week has rhythms (two hard days need an easy one) ·
14–16 he designs the ranger's *week* around a big goal (taper before the count; the athlete's
logic) · 16–18 the quiet flip: "you plan the ranger's energy well — the same dials exist for you,"
offered once, privately, never tracked.
**Varies.** Seasons change the dials (short winter days, hot-noon summers); expedition weeks stress
them.
**Flags.** Design hypothesis — but the *content* is doc 04 §6's vetted minimum (sleep/movement/food
as fuel for what he wants to do) taught through fiction instead of through his body. The bridge to
self stays an invitation, never a mirror with numbers.

### Fuel Garden — the young-band on-ramp
`engine 03` · `Model B (presence)` · bands 8–12 · seed: 04 §5
**Loop.** Rest and meals grow the day's energy like a small garden; tend it and the quest goes
better. Absorbed into The Ranger's Day as its 8–12 face.
**Flags.** Habit *presence* only, never weight/calorie anything (doc 04 §5).

### Move Like the Animals — the game asks to be put down
`engine 03` · `Model B (presence)` · bands 8–12 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** Between screen scenes, thirty-second movement snacks in the fiction: leap the brook like a
roe deer, stand heron-still on one leg, badger-crawl under the table. The tablet goes face-down; a
chime ends the snack. Parent co-play doubles the fun (and the evidence — co-regulation, doc 04 §7).
**Grows.** 8–10 mimicry snacks · 10–12 he invents the animal-move of the week and teaches it
(authorship flip, small); balance holds lengthen (heron → heron-with-eyes-closed).
**Varies.** Season packs (frog spring, stag autumn); indoor/outdoor variants; "quiet moves" for
late evening.
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; WHO ~60 min/day is the backdrop (doc 04 §6) but the game never counts
minutes — it just *causes* movement and marks presence. No fitness framing, no body talk.

### The Sleep Cabin — wind-down as craft
`engine 03` · `Model B (presence)` · bands 10–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** Design the ranger's evening ritual: last lantern, kit laid out for morning, one page of
the almanac, dark cabin. A well-built ritual gives tomorrow's ranger his best day (the energy loop
pays it out). Then, optionally, privately: build *your own* wind-down from the same parts — screens
out is one card among several, never a sermon.
**Grows (L4).** 10–12 assemble the ranger's ritual from cards · 12–14 notice which real-life card
he already has and which he'd like (pick one, try a week, felt-difference reflection) · 14–18 the
teen redesign: bedtimes shift, autonomy is the lever — he owns the ritual, revises it after late
nights, no guilt loop.
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; consistent bed/wake and screens-before-sleep are doc 04 §6's vetted
levers. Presence-only; the game never knows his actual bedtime.

### Attention Campfire — single-tasking, scored as the fire
`engine 03 (+01 for reps)` · `B (+A)` · bands 8–18 · seed: 04 §5
**Loop.** Tend one fire; sparks (distractions) drift by; feeding the fire — not chasing sparks —
builds a tall steady flame. The *fire* has a streak; the child never does.
**Grows (L3, L5).** 8–10 short tends, obvious sparks · 10–12 longer holds, sneakier sparks
(interesting ones — the honest kind) · 12–14 the noticing upgrade: catching the *pull* to switch
and returning counts as skill, not failure (attention is re-aiming, not never-wavering) · 14–18
merges into One-Tab Trail's real-work blocks.
**Flags.** Attention reps extend the EF engines (PLAN §5.9); the staircase already knows how to
tune hold-length.

### One-Tab Trail — distraction inoculation
`engine 01 + 03` · `A + B` · bands 12–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** A real focus task in-game (map-drawing, sorting the seed ledger) while the game itself
sends gentle fake notifications (a bird flaps by, a message chirps). The rep: notice the pull, name
it ("spark"), return. Staircase knob: distractor frequency/attractiveness vs hold length.
**Grows (L3, L9).** 12–14 in-game only; the return-move is the scored unit (the fire's, not his) ·
14–16 the felt experiment: one real homework block with the tablet in another room vs one with it
beside him — *felt difference*, logged privately, once (doc 04 §3's 15–16 rung verbatim) · 16–18
he engineers his own focus environment and writes the anti-derail If-Then cards himself (area 04
seam).
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; single-tasking/attention-protection is doc 04 §4's "reasonable"
tier — sold as *protecting attention from engineered distraction* (the AI-era headwind, PLAN §5.9),
never as a general focus-trainer claim (doc 06 §8: far-transfer ≈ 0; the EF line holds).

### The Fuel Test — n=1 felt experiments (parent-in-loop)
`engine 03` · `Model B (private reflection)` · bands 12–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** One variable, one week, felt result: breakfast vs none on practice mornings; water bottle
at the desk vs not; movement snack before homework vs not. Predict first (Sure-O-Meter seam), then
the felt-difference note. The parent co-designs; food experiments are *addition-shaped only* (try
adding breakfast — never restriction-shaped).
**Grows (L9, L10).** 12–14 pick from safe pre-built experiments · 14–16 design his own with the
CVS discipline (one thing at a time — area 01's move, on his own life) · 16–18 the Training Plan
crossover: does sleep move the practice curve? (area 04).
**Flags.** Design hypothesis with a **hard guard**: no weighing, no calories, no restriction, no
appearance talk, ever (doc 04 §6); food experiments need the parent switch on. If the standalone
health brief lands rules stricter than these, the brief wins.

### The Screen Bargain — attention autonomy, teen band
`engine 03` · `Model B (private, presence-only)` · bands 13–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** Not a parental control — a self-experiment: *he* sets tonight's intention ("one episode,
then the model kit"), an If-Then card guards the weak moment, and tomorrow a ten-second private
note: did the evening go how *he* wanted? The game never sees his screen time; it only holds his
own intention next to his own reflection.
**Grows.** 13–15 evening-scale bargains · 15–18 he identifies his personal "attention leaks" and
designs around them; the mechanic retires itself when the cards feel unnecessary (scaffold
removal, doc 03 §8).
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; the riskiest card in the doc — it dies the moment it feels like
surveillance. Private by construction, self-set only, no parent view, no streaks, off-switch
permanent. If it can't be built to feel like *his* tool, cut it.

### Energy Weather Map — design your own day
`engine 03` · `Model B` · bands 15–18 · new — design hypothesis
**Loop.** A week of optional two-tap check-ins (morning/afternoon/evening: sunny/cloudy/foggy — his
energy, his words) sketches his personal weather map: when is he sharpest? Then the payoff: put the
hard thing at the sharp time for one week; felt difference, once.
**Grows.** 15–16 the map + one placement experiment · 16–18 full design-your-day/week around real
commitments (the doc 04 §3 17–18 rung: interest > willpower; recovery and rhythm as the base
layer).
**Flags.** Design hypothesis; this is the *one* card where the child's own state is logged — so:
his words, his icons, no interpretation by the game, no trends shown unless he asks, delete
anytime (doc 06 §9.7), never linked to any reward. If that boundary can't hold in build, fold the
idea into private paper instead.

## What was deliberately NOT added

For the record (the doc 04 §6 + tone lines rule them out): step/calorie/sleep *tracking* of the
child in any form · integration with wearables · seated-meditation drills as a spine (MYRIAD null,
doc 04 §9) · "healthy choices" moralising in the fiction (the kiosk sells appelflappen without
commentary) · any consequence where the *child's* real-life behaviour changes the *game's* rewards
(that inverts the avatar principle and builds a surveillance loop).

## Cross-area seams (build once)

- **Interoception**: body-signal noticing lives in area 10 (perception) and regulation in area 06;
  this area consumes both (the battery check is a Body-Weather-Vane reading).
- **If-Then cards** (04) are the attention-protection workhorse here.
- **CVS → Fuel Test → Training Plan** (01 → 09 → 04) is the learning-to-learn chain.
- **The Ranger's Day** feeds every expedition: expedition planning includes energy planning (the
  pacing beat is where this area rides along invisibly).
- **When the standalone health brief lands** (doc 04 §6), it re-vets every card here — this doc is
  explicitly provisional until then.
